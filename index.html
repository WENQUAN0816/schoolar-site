About

I work on machine learning and natural-language methods for measuring the built environment. My published work uses NLP and SciBERT topic modelling, LLM-assisted indicator construction, meta-learning and attentive neural processes, LSTM model predictive control, generative models, 3D point-cloud segmentation with transfer learning, and multi-criteria decision analysis (fuzzy Delphi, AHP, DEMATEL).

PhD in Interior Design, Universiti Sains Malaysia, awarded 2026 — the programme sits in an architecture school, but the research itself is computational. Twenty-three journal articles, fourteen as first author, eleven of them SCIE/SSCI with a cumulative impact factor of 57.4 (2025 JCR) and ten in Q1. Principal Investigator of five funded projects.

Research interests

  • NLP of built-environment evidence. SciBERT topic modelling, LLM-assisted indicator construction, retrieval over policy, survey and assessment text.
  • Machine learning for spatial assessment. Deep learning on 3D point clouds, transfer and meta-learning under small samples, interpretability with SHAP.
  • Decision-analytic weighting. Fuzzy Delphi, AHP and DEMATEL for indicator systems under expert uncertainty, with consistency control.
  • Software realisation. Rule engines, versioned scoring specifications, regression tests, offline-first mobile data capture, and public web demos.
