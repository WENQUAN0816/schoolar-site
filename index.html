Teaching profile

My teaching is organised around the full design cycle: observing people and places, defining a problem, developing alternatives, testing decisions, and communicating a resolved proposal.

Studio and design education

I support students from early concept development to final presentation, with emphasis on spatial reasoning, user needs, design critique, and clear visual and written communication.

Technical and digital learning

I teach students to connect drawing, surveying, CAD/BIM, rendering, materials, and digital tools with practical design decisions rather than treating software as an isolated skill.

Courses and teaching areas

  • Architectural and interior design studio
  • Existing-building renovation and adaptive reuse
  • Architectural investigation and surveying
  • Engineering drawing and building structures
  • Materials, rendering, CAD, and BIM fundamentals
  • Architectural space design and design history

Student support

  • Individual feedback and structured studio reviews
  • Portfolio and final-project preparation
  • Interdisciplinary design problem framing
  • Competition and exhibition project mentoring
  • Research-informed design documentation
